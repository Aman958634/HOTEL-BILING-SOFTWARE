# RestoSphere First Five Client Plan

## Objective

Acquire five qualified restaurant trials and convert successful trials into paid subscriptions. This is a planning document; it contains no fabricated lead records.

## Market Focus

- Palanpur: first priority
- Nearby North Gujarat: second priority
- Selected Ahmedabad prospects: third priority

## Selection Criteria

Each trial candidate should have:

- One to three outlets
- Accessible owner/decision maker
- Active dine-in operation
- A real billing, KOT, table, payment, inventory, reporting, or staff-control pain
- Willingness to train staff
- No mandatory unsupported integration
- A realistic go-live window

## Trial Offer

- 15-day supported trial
- Setup fee waived for the first five approved customers
- Growth at ₹1,499/month for the first three paid months
- Standard Growth at ₹1,999/month afterward
- Custom work quoted separately
- Hardware client-supplied/separate

## Execution

1. Build genuine public-business prospect records.
2. Score each lead.
3. Contact and qualify.
4. Run a focused demo.
5. Start trial only after client scope and decision-maker are confirmed.
6. Complete onboarding and training.
7. Review Day 3 and Day 7 usage.
8. Discuss conversion on Day 10–15.
9. Record Won/Lost and reason.

## Success Criteria

- Five qualified trials started.
- No unresolved P0/P1 correctness, security, payment, or tenant issue.
- Staff can complete the core POS → KOT/KDS → billing flow.
- Decision maker attends a value review.
- Commercial next step is recorded for every trial.
