# RestoSphere BDE Daily Checklist

## Morning

- [ ] Review every active lead and confirm a next action/date.
- [ ] Prioritize HOT leads and trials ending within seven days.
- [ ] Prepare today’s demos using a safe demo tenant.
- [ ] Check client onboarding/support issues before new outreach.
- [ ] Prepare 10–20 personalized business contacts, not bulk spam.

## New Outreach

- [ ] Use public business information or voluntarily provided contact details.
- [ ] Confirm restaurant/cafe/QSR/hotel fit before contacting.
- [ ] Use the appropriate WhatsApp/call/walk-in template.
- [ ] Record Lead ID, source, pain, and next action immediately.

## Follow-Ups

- [ ] Follow up with replies first.
- [ ] Follow up with qualified prospects and active trials.
- [ ] Do not repeatedly contact a prospect who has declined.
- [ ] Keep objections and lost reasons factual.

## Walk-Ins / Demos

- [ ] Avoid peak service disruption.
- [ ] Confirm decision-maker or useful operational contact.
- [ ] Demonstrate Table → Order → KOT/KDS → Bill → Payment visibility.
- [ ] Do not use real customer data or real payment credentials.
- [ ] Classify outcome: Trial Ready, Follow-up, or Not Fit.

## Trial Clients

- [ ] Day 0 onboarding complete.
- [ ] Day 1 go-live/support check.
- [ ] Day 3 usage follow-up.
- [ ] Day 7 value review.
- [ ] Day 10 commercial discussion.
- [ ] Day 13 reminder.
- [ ] Day 15 decision.

## End of Day

- [ ] Update pipeline stage, notes, owner, and next action.
- [ ] Record demos booked/completed.
- [ ] Record trials started/active.
- [ ] Record commercial discussions and outcomes.
- [ ] Record objections and lost reasons.
- [ ] Escalate any P0/P1 client issue immediately.
- [ ] Prepare tomorrow’s first three priorities.
