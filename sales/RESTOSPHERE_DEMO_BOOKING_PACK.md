## Demo Opening

“Thank you for the time. I will first understand the workflow you described, then show only the relevant path: login/dashboard, table or order, KOT/KDS, billing, payment visibility, and reports. Please stop me where the workflow differs from your operation.”

## Fifteen-Minute Flow

1. Login, outlet, and dashboard context.
2. Table or takeaway Order.
3. KOT/KDS handoff.
4. Bill and payment status.
5. Reports.
6. One or two relevant modules only: inventory, QR, CRM/loyalty, staff, or multi-outlet scope.
7. Ask whether the workflow addresses the confirmed problem.

Do not claim that offline operation authoritatively creates Orders, KOTs, payments, inventory changes, or final totals. Do not claim live Zomato/Swiggy integration unless separately verified and configured.

## Demo Closing

“Based on what you saw, is this workflow worth testing in your restaurant?”

If yes:

“Let us confirm the restaurant scope, decision-maker involvement, staff willingness, onboarding availability, and a proposed trial start date. The approved supported trial is 15 days; we can review the actual workflow before discussing continuation.”
