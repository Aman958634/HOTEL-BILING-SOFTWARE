# RestoSphere BDE Objection Card

**Use during live conversations. Keep replies factual and return to discovery.**

**We already have software.**  
“That may be working well. What, if anything, would you improve in billing, KOT, tables, reports, inventory, or outlet visibility? If there is no meaningful gap, I will not force a demo.”

**Petpooja is established.**  
“Petpooja is an established restaurant-tech product. What do you like about it, and what would you improve? RestoSphere is worth comparing only where our supported workflow and onboarding fit your need.”

**Price is high.**  
“Starter begins at ₹999/month and Growth is ₹1,999/month. The right recommendation depends on your scope. May I first understand the workflow so we do not quote modules you do not need?”

**We are busy.**  
“Understood. What business time would be appropriate for a two-minute question or a 15-minute demo? I will not interrupt service.”

**Call later.**  
“Certainly. What date and business time should I note? I will use the same published business contact.”

**Send details on WhatsApp.**  
“Sure. May I send a short summary to this business number? I will keep it to the relevant Order, KOT, billing, and reports workflow. No passwords or payment credentials should be shared there.”

**Owner is not here.**  
“Understood. Who handles billing or software questions today? If appropriate, what business time should I call back? I do not need a private number.”

**We do not need inventory.**  
“That is fine. We can keep discovery focused on POS, tables, KOT/KDS, billing, and reports. Inventory can remain out of scope.”

**Internet issue?**  
“When connectivity is lost, the current flow can preserve bounded device-local pending order intent for manual retry. It does not create authoritative offline Orders, KOTs, table occupancy, payments, inventory changes, or final totals.”

**Do you have Zomato/Swiggy?**  
“We do not claim live Zomato or Swiggy integration unless provider setup, credentials, mapping, webhooks, and validation are complete. That requirement can be reviewed separately.”

**Your company/product is new.**  
“That is a fair concern. We use a controlled supported trial, defined onboarding, staff training, support escalation, and transparent limitations so you can evaluate the actual workflow before committing.”

## Never Say

- “You have an inventory problem.”
- “Your current POS is bad.”
- “We are better than Petpooja.”
- “Everything works offline.”
- “We have live Zomato/Swiggy integration.”
- “The trial or discount is automatic for everyone.”
- “Your demo or trial is booked” unless the prospect agreed to a date and time.
