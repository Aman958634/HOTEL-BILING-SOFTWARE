# RestoSphere Client Acquisition Playbook

## Market Priority

Tier 1: Palanpur  
Tier 2: nearby North Gujarat cities  
Tier 3: selected Ahmedabad prospects  
Tier 4: other Gujarat cities after early evidence

The first objective is direct owner access, practical demos, quick support, and high-quality pilot feedback.

## ICP

Prioritize:

- Independent restaurants
- Cafes/QSRs
- Small hotel restaurants
- One-to-three outlet groups
- Active dine-in operations
- Manual/weak KOT or billing workflows
- Owners willing to evaluate a new system

Avoid early prospects requiring major custom development or mandatory unsupported integrations.

## Ethical Lead Sources

- Public Google Business listings
- Public restaurant websites
- Public business Instagram/Facebook pages
- LinkedIn business pages
- Local directories and associations
- Referrals
- Walk-ins
- Existing ANOVA business network

Do not scrape private information or send automated spam.

## Qualification Score

Score every prospect out of 100:

- Operational pain: 25
- Decision-maker access: 20
- Purchase timeline: 15
- Current POS dissatisfaction: 15
- RestoSphere fit: 15
- Trial willingness: 10

HOT: 75–100  
WARM: 50–74  
COLD: below 50

## Pipeline

NEW LEAD → CONTACTED → REPLIED → QUALIFIED → DEMO BOOKED → DEMO COMPLETED → TRIAL OFFERED → TRIAL STARTED → TRIAL ACTIVE → COMMERCIAL DISCUSSION → WON

Alternative exits: NOT INTERESTED, FOLLOW UP LATER, LOST.

Every active lead must have an owner, next action, and next action date.

## Outreach Sequence

1. Personalized first contact.
2. Short discovery conversation.
3. Book a focused demo.
4. Demonstrate only the prospect’s relevant workflow.
5. Offer the 15-day trial when fit is proven.
6. Onboard and train.
7. Review value on Day 7.
8. Discuss the Growth plan by default when scope fits.
9. Quote with setup/customization boundaries.
10. Convert or record a factual lost reason.

## First Contact

WhatsApp, call, or walk-in must identify ANOVA Technologies, mention one relevant restaurant problem, and ask permission for a short demo. Do not dump the full feature list or contact prospects aggressively during peak service.

Call opening:

“Hello, I’m [NAME] from ANOVA Technologies. We help restaurants connect POS, KOT, billing, and operations. I’m calling to understand how your team handles Orders and KOT today. If there is a genuine gap, may I book a short demo?”

Discovery should cover outlets, order volume, current POS, KOT process, KDS, inventory, payments, staff, QR interest, CRM/loyalty, reports, decision maker, pain, and timeline.

## Demo Qualification

Run the full demo when the prospect has a real operational need, a decision maker or strong influencer is engaged, the product fits, and there is willingness to evaluate. Otherwise use a short demo or move the lead to Follow Up Later.

## Conversion

Trial → value review → remaining objection → plan recommendation → quotation → decision → Won/Lost. Review actual operational value before discounting. Pass the winning handoff to Implementation with scope, dates, client contact, selected plan, known risks, and promises made.

## First-Fifty Allocation

- Palanpur: 20 prospect records to be filled from genuine public research
- Nearby North Gujarat: 15 prospect records
- Ahmedabad/selected Gujarat: 15 prospect records

This is an allocation plan only. No fake businesses or contact details are preloaded.

## First-Five Goal

The first five qualified trial restaurants should be selected for:

- Accessible owner/decision maker
- Real POS/KOT/table pain
- Manageable setup
- Staff willingness to learn
- No mandatory unsupported integration
- Geographic supportability

## Weekly Review

Report real values only:

- New leads
- Qualified leads
- Demos booked/completed
- Trials started/active
- Commercial discussions
- Won/lost
- Lost reasons
- Next-week priorities
