# RestoSphere Pricing Guide

These are ANOVA-recommended commercial prices, not values hardcoded as product entitlements. Confirm final commercial terms before quoting.

## Recommended Packages

| Package | Monthly | Annual | Suggested fit | Code catalog limits | Included direction |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Starter | ₹999/month | ₹9,990/year | Single outlet, small team | 10 users, 20 tables, 100 menu items, 2,000 orders/month | POS, menu, tables, billing, basic reports, staff roles |
| Growth | ₹1,999/month | ₹19,990/year | Busy single outlet, cafe/QSR | 50 users, 80 tables, 500 menu items, 10,000 orders/month | Starter plus KOT/KDS, QR, inventory/recipes, CRM/loyalty, advanced reports |
| Pro | ₹2,999/month | ₹29,990/year | Larger operation / multi-outlet base | 200 users, 300 tables, 2,000 menu items, 100,000 orders/month | Growth plus broader analytics, multi-outlet architecture, central-kitchen workflows where applicable, priority onboarding |

Annual pricing is a commercial recommendation with an approximate two-month saving. It is not a promise of a fixed product limit unless confirmed in the signed quote.

The limits above are based on the current Basic, Professional, and Enterprise plan catalog. Confirm the active plan catalog before issuing a quote. Pro is a base subscription for the agreed primary scope; additional outlets should be quoted separately rather than described as unlimited.

## Additional Outlet Rule

Recommended commercial rule: add `₹750/month per additional outlet` for Starter/Growth and `₹1,000/month per additional outlet` for Pro, subject to implementation scope. This is a commercial recommendation, not a hardcoded billing rule. Confirm the final outlet count, users, tables, menu scope, and support plan in the quote.

## Fifteen-Day Trial

- One 15-day trial per approved restaurant onboarding.
- No automatic charge unless explicitly agreed and technically supported.
- Trial includes onboarding scope, training, and a conversion review.
- The trial should be evaluated on real operational usage, not vanity logins.

## Pilot Offer: First 5–10 Customers

Recommended limited offer:

- Setup fee waived for the first five customers.
- One outlet/menu/table/staff onboarding included.
- Growth plan at ₹1,499/month for the first three paid months, then standard renewal pricing.
- Offer expiry: define a real date in the quote; do not make it permanent.
- Additional outlets, custom workflows, special reports, hardware, and integrations quoted separately.

## Setup Fee

Recommended standard setup ranges:

- Single-outlet setup: ₹5,000–₹10,000 one time
- Multi-outlet setup: ₹10,000–₹25,000 one time
- Menu/table/staff data preparation beyond the agreed scope: quote separately
- First-pilot setup: waive or reduce only as a documented early-customer offer

## Customization Rule

STANDARD PRODUCT: subscription plus agreed onboarding.  
CUSTOM REQUIREMENT: separate written quote with scope, estimate, acceptance criteria, and support boundary.

Examples:

- Custom report
- Special integration
- Hardware/printer workflow
- Bespoke approval flow
- Third-party API integration

Do not include unlimited custom work in a subscription.

## Commercial Guardrails

Do not promise:

- Guaranteed revenue growth
- Guaranteed uptime without an agreed SLA
- Instant payment settlement
- Full offline payment/KOT authority
- Live Zomato/Swiggy integration unless connected
- Enterprise capacity based on local tests
- Tax/legal advice
