# RestoSphere Objection Handling

## “We already use another POS.”

That may be working well. The useful question is where the current workflow still creates friction: billing, KOT, tables, outlet control, inventory, reconciliation, or reports. We can run a focused 15-day pilot against one agreed workflow rather than ask you to change everything at once.

## “Petpooja is established.”

Petpooja is an established category player. RestoSphere should be considered when direct onboarding, implementation attention, a modern Order → KOT workflow, CRM/loyalty, multi-outlet controls, or a more tailored pilot matter to you. We will not claim a larger support ecosystem or live integration maturity where we cannot prove it.

## “Your company is new.”

That is a fair concern. We address it with a controlled pilot, a defined onboarding checklist, clear support escalation, and transparent limitations. You can evaluate the operational workflow before committing to broader rollout.

## “The price is high.”

Let’s compare the price with the problem being solved: duplicate manual work, payment reconciliation, missed KOTs, weak outlet visibility, or stock uncertainty. We can start with the package that matches your actual scope rather than sell modules you do not need.

## “We do not need inventory.”

Then keep the pilot focused on POS, tables, KOT/KDS, billing, and reports. Inventory and recipes can remain out of scope until they become useful.

## “My staff will not learn it.”

That is why the pilot includes role-based training: waiter, kitchen, cashier, manager, and admin workflows. We use one test Order and one real operating flow before opening service.

## “What if the internet goes down?”

The current offline flow preserves local order intent and supports manual retry when connectivity returns. It does not create authoritative offline payments, KOTs, inventory changes, or final Order totals. We disclose that boundary clearly.

## “What if the software stops?”

The deployment has health/readiness endpoints, structured logs, request IDs, shutdown handling, and a support runbook. Provider backups and external monitoring still need to be verified as part of deployment operations.

## “Do you support payments?”

The platform supports cash, configured digital payment flows, partial/split payment paths, idempotency, and reconciliation architecture. Live gateway credentials, webhooks, and provider settlement behavior must be configured and validated separately.

## “Can you integrate Zomato/Swiggy?”

There is provider-adapter architecture, but we do not claim live Zomato/Swiggy production integration unless provider onboarding, credentials, webhooks, mapping, and validation are complete.

## “Can I use multiple outlets?”

Yes, multi-outlet scoping and outlet-authorized operations are supported. We begin with a controlled outlet scope and expand after the pilot is stable.

## “Can I cancel?”

We can explain the commercial terms in the quote. The 15-day trial is a product trial; subscription cancellation, renewal, refunds, and payment terms must follow the approved commercial agreement rather than an informal promise.

## “Can you customize it?”

Standard product capability is covered by subscription and agreed onboarding. Custom reports, special workflows, hardware, and external integrations are quoted separately with written scope and acceptance criteria.
