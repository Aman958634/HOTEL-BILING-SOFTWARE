# RestoSphere

## One operational flow for the restaurant team

RestoSphere helps restaurants connect POS Orders, tables, KOT/KDS, billing, payment visibility, inventory, staff permissions, QR ordering, CRM, loyalty, and reports in one controlled workflow.

## Why restaurant teams use it

- Create Orders and send the right context to the kitchen.
- Keep tables, bills, payments, and reports connected.
- Give staff the access they need for their role and outlet.
- Track inventory and recipes when the operation needs it.
- Add QR ordering, CRM, loyalty, and multi-outlet visibility as the business grows.
- Start with ANOVA onboarding, training, and a focused pilot.

## 15-day free trial

We configure an agreed restaurant/outlet scope, train the team, run a safe Order → KOT/KDS → billing check, and review the value with you before continuation.

## Built for a practical rollout

Start with one outlet and the workflows that matter most. Expand after the team is comfortable and the pilot evidence is clear.

## Honest boundaries

Offline mode preserves order intent for manual sync; it is not full offline payment or KOT authority. Live external aggregator integrations require separate provider onboarding and validation. Production-scale capacity is evaluated through controlled operations rather than assumed from local benchmarks.
