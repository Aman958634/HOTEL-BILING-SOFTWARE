# RestoSphere 15-Day Trial Conversion

## Trial Timeline

### Day 0: Onboarding

- Confirm client information, outlet, menu, tables, staff, roles, and go-live date.
- Deliver access securely.
- Schedule admin/staff training.
- Define one measurable trial success outcome.

### Day 1: Go-Live

- Run the approved test Order → KOT → KDS → bill flow.
- Keep trained support available.
- Record issues by request ID/order number.

### Days 2–5: Usage Support

Ask what staff used, where they hesitated, and whether any order/KOT/payment/table issue occurred. Resolve operational blockers before adding scope.

### Day 7: Mid-Trial Review

Review:

- POS usage
- KOT/KDS usage
- Billing/payment usage
- Staff comfort
- Reports reviewed
- Remaining blocker
- Decision maker confidence

### Day 10: Commercial Discussion

Discuss the plan that matches actual outlet scope. Ask what must be resolved before paid continuation. Do not pressure the client or imply automatic charging.

### Day 13: Final Reminder

Confirm the trial end date, selected plan, setup/customization scope, and handover of any open questions.

### Day 15: Trial Ending

Review value, plan, next payment/setup step, and support contact. Do not claim deletion unless the actual product behavior says so.

### Day 16+: Expired / Upgrade

Explain that the trial has ended and provide the approved upgrade path. Preserve the client’s data/account expectations accurately.

## Trial Success Criteria

A successful trial has evidence that:

- Staff used POS for real pilot workflows.
- Kitchen used KOT/KDS.
- Billing was reviewed.
- The decision maker attended the review.
- No unresolved critical correctness, payment, security, or tenant issue remains.
- The client can name the operational problem RestoSphere improved.

## Messages

### Day 0 WhatsApp

“Hello [NAME], your RestoSphere 15-day pilot for [RESTAURANT/OUTLET] is ready. We’ll guide your team through POS, KOT/KDS, billing, and the modules in your agreed scope. We will share access through the approved secure channel and confirm training at [TIME]. Please do not send passwords or payment credentials over WhatsApp.”

### Day 3 Follow-Up

“Hi [NAME], how is the team finding RestoSphere so far? We’d like to know what is working, where staff need help, and whether any POS, KOT, billing, table, or inventory question is blocking service.”

### Day 7 Review

“Hi [NAME], can we review the first week of the pilot? We’ll look at the workflows your team actually used, answer questions, and agree the remaining steps before the trial ends.”

### Day 10 Conversion Discussion

“Your trial is entering its final phase. Based on the workflows you used, we recommend the [PLAN] plan for [OUTLET COUNT]. What remaining question should we resolve before you decide on continuation?”

### Day 13 Reminder

“Reminder: the RestoSphere trial for [RESTAURANT] ends on [DATE]. We can review the selected plan, onboarding scope, and next support steps before then.”

### Day 15 Final Message

“Your RestoSphere trial has ended today. Thank you for testing the workflow with us. We’re ready to review continuation on the [PLAN] plan or answer any remaining questions before you decide.”

### Day 16+ Expired Message

“The RestoSphere trial for [RESTAURANT] has ended. Your account status and data handling follow the product’s current subscription behavior. Contact us to review the available upgrade/continuation path.”

## Conversion Call

1. Ask what changed operationally.
2. Ask which workflow the team used most.
3. Ask what remains unresolved.
4. Recommend one plan based on scope.
5. Confirm monthly/annual price and setup/custom work separately.
6. Confirm decision maker and next date.
7. Record won, continue, or lost reason.
