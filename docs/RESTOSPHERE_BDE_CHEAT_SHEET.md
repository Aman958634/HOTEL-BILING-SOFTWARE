# RestoSphere BDE Cheat Sheet

## What Is RestoSphere?

A restaurant operations platform that connects POS Orders, tables, KOT/KDS, billing, payments, inventory, staff permissions, QR ordering, CRM/loyalty, reports, and multi-outlet operations.

## Best First Customer

A single-outlet restaurant, cafe/QSR, or small hotel restaurant with an engaged owner and a clear operational pain. Start local: Palanpur and nearby Gujarat cities.

## Top Benefits

1. One Order workflow from front of house to kitchen.
2. Better table, bill, and payment visibility.
3. Staff access matched to roles/outlets.
4. Inventory/recipe visibility when needed.
5. QR, CRM, loyalty, and reports without a fragmented workflow.

## Demo Flow

Login → dashboard → table → Order → KOT/KDS → bill/payment → QR → inventory/CRM/report → trial.

## Packages

- Starter: ₹999/month recommended for a small single outlet.
- Growth: ₹1,999/month recommended for a growing outlet/cafe.
- Pro: ₹2,999/month recommended for multi-outlet or broader operations.
- Current catalog guide: Starter 10 users/20 tables/100 items; Growth 50/80/500; Pro 200/300/2,000.
- Additional outlets are quoted separately; do not promise unlimited outlets.
- First-customer pilot offer: documented, time-limited discount; do not make it permanent.

## Trial

- Day 0 onboarding
- Day 1 go-live
- Day 3 support check
- Day 7 value review
- Day 10 plan discussion
- Day 13 reminder
- Day 15 decision

## Discovery Questions

- How many outlets and staff?
- How many orders on a normal day?
- What is the current billing/KOT process?
- Where do errors happen?
- How are payments reconciled?
- How is stock tracked?
- Who decides?
- When do you want to go live?

## Closing Question

“If we configure the agreed outlet, train the team, and prove the Order → KOT → billing workflow during the pilot, what would you need to see before continuing?”

## Never Claim

- Enterprise scale proven
- Full offline payments/KOT
- Live Zomato/Swiggy integration without provider validation
- Guaranteed revenue growth
- Guaranteed uptime without an agreed SLA
- Instant payment settlement
- Unlimited customization

## Handoff

- Trial dates
